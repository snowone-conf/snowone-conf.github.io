<p>This session is about how the desire to control the heating system from a warm apartment rather than from the basement led to byte shifting, reverse engineering with raw TCP packet listening, and long struggles with encryption and magic numbers. I will tell you how Kotlin helped with this, which solutions proved successful and which did not, and why developing your own SCADA sometimes feels more like research work.</p>
